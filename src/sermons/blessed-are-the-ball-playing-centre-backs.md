## The Beatitudes of the Back Line

> Wisdom crieth aloud in the penalty area; she raiseth her voice at the edge of the box.

<div class="beatitudes">
  <p>Blessed are <strong>the calm in possession</strong>, for they shall not pass back to a goalkeeper who standing like a mannequin of sorrow.</p>
  <p>Blessed are <strong>those who receive on the half-turn</strong>, for theirs is the kingdom of the forward pass.</p>
  <p>Blessed are <strong>the left-footed</strong>, for they shall open angles the right-footed knew not of.</p>
  <p>Blessed are <strong>those who hunger and thirst after progressive carries</strong>, for they shall break the first line and be filled.</p>
  <p>Blessed are <strong>the merciful</strong>, who step out to intercept yet leave a covering shadow, for they shall obtain defensive transition.</p>
  <p>Blessed are <strong>the pure of first touch</strong>, for they shall see through the press as through a glass window.</p>
  <p>Blessed are <strong>the peacemakers</strong>, who take the ball off a panicking full-back and slow the very pulsing of the match.</p>
  <p>Blessed are <strong>those who are persecuted for the outswinging diagonal</strong>, for theirs is the applause of the away end and the highlight reel.</p>
</div>

## Woe Unto the Hoofers

> The letter killeth; the diagonal giveth life.

But woe unto you, disciples of the agricultural clearance, defenders of the if-in-doubt orthodoxy — for the press hath studied your hoof, and the second ball falleth unto the enemy as rain upon the just and the unjust alike. Woe also unto the over-trusting, the dweller-upon-the-ball, the centre-back who attempteth the Cruyff turn upon his own six-yard box: for your punishment shall be swift, televised, and looped forever. There is a fine line between the libero and the liability, and that line is called <em>timing</em>.

<div class="pull-quote">
  <p>Every great defence begins as an act of creation: first the ball, then the world.</p>
</div>

Consider the numbers as one considereth the lilies: in the great leagues of Europe, the average centre-back now completes more passes in a season than many a number ten of old. The game has been baptised, and the defence has become the first attack. Rejoice, therefore, and be exceedingly glad — for great is your xG buildup in heaven.

<div class="benediction">
  <p>May your half-turns be smooth, your diagonals measure forty yards on the dot matrix of destiny, and your clearing headers travel only when they are truly necessary. <strong>Amen. And may you never be nutmegged near your own area.</strong></p>
</div>
